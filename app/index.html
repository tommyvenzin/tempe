Tempe Orders

Repair Orders and Order Check on your phone.

Install on iPhone

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open Tempe from your home screen and pair it below.

Needs Safari on iOS 16.4 or newer. The home-screen app keeps its own data, separate from Safari, so you pair it and enter the passphrase again inside the app.

Already paired?

If the desk PC's pairing was reset, Repair Orders says "pair again". Just scan the new QR above. Forgetting is only needed to unpair this phone.

Before first use

  • Be on the shop Wi-Fi. The desk PC helper is only reachable on the shop network.
  • Install the desk PC's certificate and fully trust it: the pairing page on the desk PC shows a QR for the certificate. After installing, go to Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings and switch it on.
  • Open Order Check inside the app and enter the team passphrase once. Home-screen apps don't share Safari's saved data.
